CALORIE TRACKING

Developer: Balance Apps

About the application

CALORIE TRACKING is a calorie and nutrition tracking application designed to help users record their body weight, meals, calories, nutrients and physical activity.

The application supports local data storage and optional Google Account sign-in with Google Drive backup and restore.

Google Account and Google Drive

Users may sign in with their Google Account and, if they choose, use the application's Google Drive backup and restore functionality.

Application backup data is stored in the application's private Google Drive app data folder (appDataFolder).

Privacy

For information about how CALORIE TRACKING handles personal information, Google Account information, Google Drive backup data, advertising and other services, please read our Privacy Policy.
