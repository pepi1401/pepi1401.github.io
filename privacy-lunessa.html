LUNESSA Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how LUNESSA (“we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the LUNESSA mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Use

LUNESSA is designed to help users track nutrition, calories, body weight, menstrual cycle data, symptoms, workouts, and wellness information.

Most user data is stored locally on the user’s device and is not transmitted to our servers.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Application uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its partners may collect device information, advertising IDs, and app interaction data to provide personalized ads.

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and Switzerland will be asked for consent regarding personalized advertising.

3. Log Data

In case of an app error, we may collect technical Log Data such as: IP address, device name, operating system version, app configuration, and diagnostic statistics.

4. Service Providers

We may use third-party services for advertising, analytics, error reporting, and app improvement.

5. Security

We strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting user data, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Children’s Privacy

The Application is not intended for children under 13 years old.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page.

8. Manage Ad Preferences

Users may manage advertising preferences and consent choices through the app settings or Android device settings.
