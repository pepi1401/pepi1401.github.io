Privacy Policy – CALORIE TRACKING

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Application: CALORIE TRACKING
Package ID: com.caloriestracking.app
Developer: Balance Apps

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how CALORIE TRACKING handles information when you use the application. We respect your privacy and aim to collect and process only the information necessary to provide the application's functionality, display advertising where applicable, and comply with legal requirements.

2. Information stored in the application

CALORIE TRACKING may allow you to enter and store information such as:

  • body weight;
  • calorie and nutrient targets;
  • foods and meals entered by you;
  • daily calorie and nutrient history;
  • physical activity and step information, where the relevant device permissions and integrations are enabled;
  • other settings needed for the application's features.

This information is used to calculate and display your calorie, nutrition and activity information inside the application.

3. Local storage

Information entered into CALORIE TRACKING may be stored locally on your device using the application's local storage mechanisms. The application does not require you to create an online account in order to use its basic calorie-tracking functionality.

If a feature or future version of the application sends information to an online service, the applicable privacy information will be updated accordingly.

4. Health and activity information

Where you enable step or activity tracking, CALORIE TRACKING may access relevant activity information provided by Android/device health and activity services, subject to the permissions you grant. Such information is used to display activity and calorie-related information within the application.

Health and activity information is not sold by Balance Apps.

5. Advertising and Google AdMob

CALORIE TRACKING may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads). Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, diagnostics, and information related to advertising interactions as described in their applicable policies and depending on your consent and regional requirements.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and other regions where consent is required, the application uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present the applicable privacy and consent choices.

6. Privacy Settings / Consent choices

Where required, users can review or change their advertising privacy choices through the Privacy Settings option inside CALORIE TRACKING.

The in-app Privacy Settings option uses Google's privacy options interface. It is separate from this Privacy Policy page.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

The application itself may use local storage and similar technical mechanisms to remember your settings and tracking information. Advertising services such as Google AdMob may use identifiers and similar technologies according to their applicable policies and your consent choices.

8. Data sharing

Balance Apps does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by third-party service providers when necessary to provide services used by the application, such as advertising and related consent management.

9. Children's privacy

CALORIE TRACKING is not specifically directed at children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information in a way that is not appropriate, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

10. Your choices

You can control the information you enter into the application by editing or removing it through the available application features. You can also manage applicable advertising consent choices through the in-app Privacy Settings option.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the application, services, legal requirements or data practices. The latest version will be made available at the public Privacy Policy address used by CALORIE TRACKING.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning CALORIE TRACKING, please contact the developer through the contact details provided on the application's Google Play listing.

13. Third-party privacy policies

For more information about Google's advertising and privacy practices, please refer to Google's official privacy documentation and Google AdMob policies.
