Privacy Policy - CALORIE TRACKING

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Application: CALORIE TRACKING
Package ID: com.caloriestracking.app
Developer: Balance Apps

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how CALORIE TRACKING handles information when you use the application. We respect your privacy and aim to collect and process only the information necessary to provide the application's functionality, provide optional cloud backup and restore, display advertising where applicable, and comply with legal requirements.

2. Information stored in the application

CALORIE TRACKING may allow you to enter and store information such as:

  • body weight;
  • calorie and nutrient targets;
  • foods and meals entered by you;
  • daily calorie and nutrient history;
  • physical activity and step information, where the relevant device permissions and integrations are enabled;
  • recipes and custom food information entered by you;
  • other settings needed for the application's features.

This information is used to calculate and display your calorie, nutrition and activity information inside the application.

3. Local storage

Information entered into CALORIE TRACKING may be stored locally on your device using the application's local storage mechanisms.

The basic calorie-tracking functionality does not require you to use cloud storage.

Local information may include your weight history, calorie and nutrient targets, meals, recipes, custom foods, settings and other information required for the application's features.

4. Google Account Sign-In

CALORIE TRACKING may allow you to sign in using your Google Account.

When you choose to sign in with Google, Google provides the application with information necessary to identify your Google Account, such as your email address and basic profile information, according to the permissions you grant and Google's applicable policies.

Your Google Account information is used to identify your application account and to associate your application data with the correct user.

CALORIE TRACKING does not sell your Google Account information and does not use Google Account information for advertising purposes.

5. Google Drive Backup and Restore

CALORIE TRACKING provides an optional backup and restore feature using Google Drive.

When you sign in with Google and grant the required permission, the application may store a backup of your CALORIE TRACKING application data in the application's private area of Google Drive (appDataFolder).

The backup may contain information that you have entered or generated while using the application, including:

  • body weight history;
  • calorie and nutrient targets;
  • meal and calorie history;
  • daily nutrition information;
  • recipes and custom foods;
  • application settings and other application data required for backup and restore.

The Google Drive backup is used only to allow you to restore your CALORIE TRACKING data, for example when using the application on another device and signing in with the same Google Account.

CALORIE TRACKING uses the Google Drive application data folder and does not request general access to the files stored in your Google Drive. The application does not use the Google Drive backup data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

The backup and restore functionality operates through Google APIs and is subject to Google's applicable privacy and data-use requirements.

6. Health and activity information

Where you enable step or activity tracking, CALORIE TRACKING may access relevant activity information provided by Android/device health and activity services, subject to the permissions you grant.

Such information is used to display activity and calorie-related information within the application.

Health and activity information is not sold by Balance Apps.

7. Advertising and Google AdMob

CALORIE TRACKING may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads). Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, diagnostics, and information related to advertising interactions as described in their applicable policies and depending on your consent and regional requirements.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and other regions where consent is required, the application uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present the applicable privacy and consent choices.

8. Privacy Settings / Consent choices

Where required, users can review or change their advertising privacy choices through the Privacy Settings option inside CALORIE TRACKING.

The in-app Privacy Settings option uses Google's privacy options interface. It is separate from this Privacy Policy page.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

The application itself may use local storage and similar technical mechanisms to remember your settings and tracking information.

Advertising services such as Google AdMob may use identifiers and similar technologies according to their applicable policies and your consent choices.

10. Data sharing

Balance Apps does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when necessary to provide services used by the application, such as:

  • Google Account authentication;
  • Google Drive backup and restore;
  • Google AdMob advertising;
  • Google User Messaging Platform and related consent management.

Data stored through Google Drive backup is associated with your Google Account and is processed through Google's services according to Google's applicable policies.

11. Children's privacy

CALORIE TRACKING is not specifically directed at children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information in a way that is not appropriate, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

12. Your choices

You can control the information you enter into the application by editing or removing it through the available application features.

You can choose whether to use Google Account sign-in and the optional Google Drive backup and restore functionality.

You can also manage applicable advertising consent choices through the in-app Privacy Settings option.

13. Google API Services User Data Policy

CALORIE TRACKING's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy , including its Limited Use requirements where applicable.

Information obtained through Google APIs is used only for the functionality described in this Privacy Policy, including Google Account authentication and the optional Google Drive backup and restore functionality.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the application, services, legal requirements or data practices.

The latest version will be made available at the public Privacy Policy address used by CALORIE TRACKING.

15. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning CALORIE TRACKING, please contact the developer through the contact details provided on the application's Google Play listing.

16. Third-party privacy policies

For more information about Google's services and privacy practices, please refer to Google's official documentation and privacy policies, including:
